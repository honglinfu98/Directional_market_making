\title{\textbf{SS2P2} and \textbf{PCT-S2P2}:\\
mathematical formulation and structural comparison}
\author{}
\date{16 August 2026}

\begin{abstract}
\noindent
Two marked temporal point process models on a state-space backbone. SS2P2
factorises the channel intensity as a \emph{bounded scalar rate} times a
\emph{rate-neutral} mark distribution, so the marks provably cannot move the
total rate. PCT-S2P2 replaces that factorisation with $K$ parallel per-type
towers, each decoding its own intensity, and recovers the total as their sum.
Both admit a closed-form dominating rate, so exact thinning applies to each.
They differ in one property that turns out to be decisive: SS2P2's rate is
\emph{anchored} by construction, PCT-S2P2's is only \emph{bounded}. We give
both formulations, prove the boundedness and neutrality statements, and record
the measured consequence.
\end{abstract}

\section{Common setup}

Events $(t_i, x_i)_{i\ge1}$ with $0 < t_1 < t_2 < \cdots$ and multi-hot marks
$x_i \in \{0,1\}^K$; a single event may activate several channels, so marks are
sets. Write $\Hist_t = \sigma\{(t_i,x_i) : t_i < t\}$ and $|x| = \sum_k x_k$.
Deployed: $K = 62$ channels (LO/CO $\times$ 2 sides $\times$ 10 levels, MO
$\times$ 2, IS $\times$ 2 $\times$ 10).

A marked point process is specified by its channel intensities
$\lambda_k(t) \ge 0$, with total $\Lambda(t) = \sum_k \lambda_k(t)$ and
conditional mark law $p(k \mid \Hist_t) = \lambda_k(t)/\Lambda(t)$.
The log-likelihood on $[0,T]$ is
\begin{equation}
\log\mathcal{L}
= \sum_i \log \lambda_{k_i}(t_i^-) - \int_0^T \Lambda(u)\,du .
\label{eq:ll}
\end{equation}

For simulation by Ogata's thinning we require a \emph{dominating rate}: a
constant $\ell_+$ with $\Lambda(t) \le \ell_+$ for all $t$ and all histories.
Both models below supply one in closed form; this is the property that makes
their rollouts sampleable without an adaptive bound.

\section{S2P2 backbone}

Both models read a continuous-time embedding $u(t) \in \R^H$ produced by a
stacked diagonal state-space encoder with $L$ layers of $H$ modes. With channel
embeddings $E \in \R^{K \times d_E}$,
\begin{align}
e_i &= \frac{1}{|x_i|}\sum_{k} x_{i,k} E_k, \\
\Delta^{(\ell)}(u) &= \delta^{(\ell)} \odot
  \operatorname{clamp}\!\big(\softplus(W^{(\ell)}_{\mathrm{dyn}} u),\, 0.05,\, 20\big), \\
x^{(\ell)}(t) &= \bar a \odot x^{(\ell)}(t_i^+)
  + (\mathbf 1 - \bar a) \odot W^{(\ell)}_{\mathrm{in}} u^{(\ell-1)},
  \qquad \bar a = e^{-\Delta^{(\ell)}(t - t_i)} ,
\end{align}
and $u(t)$ is the layer-normalised top-layer output. Decay rates are real and
strictly positive, so the backbone spans mixtures of real exponentials.

\section{SS2P2}

\subsection{Bounded rate head}

From the embedding $u$, with ceiling parameter $c$ (deployed $c = 6$):
\begin{equation}
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
o(u)     &= \sigma(W_o u + b_o) && \in (0,1)^H && \text{gate}\\
h(u)     &= o(u) \odot \tanh(u) && \in (-1,1)^H && \text{bounded state}\\
z_{\mathrm{raw}} &= w^{\!\top} h(u) + b && \in \R && \text{unconstrained}\\
z        &= c - \softplus(c - z_{\mathrm{raw}}) && \le c && \text{one-sided cap}\\
\lambda(t) &= s\,\softplus(z), \quad s = \softplus(\rho) && &&
\end{aligned}}
\label{eq:ss2p2rate}
\end{equation}

\subsection{Rate-neutral mark head}

The marks read the \emph{same} $u$ through a separate network and are
normalised:
\begin{equation}
\zeta(t) = \mathrm{MLP}(u(t)) \in \R^K, \qquad
p^*(k \mid t) = \frac{e^{\zeta_k(t)}}{\sum_j e^{\zeta_j(t)}}, \qquad
\lambda_k(t) = \lambda(t)\, p^*(k \mid t).
\label{eq:ss2p2mark}
\end{equation}

\begin{proposition}[Exact dominating rate]
\label{prop:ss2p2bound}
$\Lambda(t) = \lambda(t) \le s\,\softplus(c)$ for every $t$ and every history,
and the bound is attained in the limit $z_{\mathrm{raw}}\to\infty$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
$\softplus(c - z_{\mathrm{raw}}) > 0$ gives $z < c$; $\softplus$ is increasing,
so $\lambda = s\,\softplus(z) < s\,\softplus(c)$. As
$z_{\mathrm{raw}}\to\infty$, $\softplus(c-z_{\mathrm{raw}})\to 0$ and
$z \to c$. Summing~\eqref{eq:ss2p2mark} over $k$ gives $\Lambda = \lambda$.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Rate neutrality]
\label{prop:ss2p2neutral}
Let $\theta_m$ be the parameters of the mark MLP. Then
$\partial \Lambda(t) / \partial \theta_m \equiv 0$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
$\Lambda = \lambda$ by Proposition~\ref{prop:ss2p2bound}, and $\lambda$
is a function of $u$ and $(W_o,b_o,w,b,\rho)$ only; $\theta_m$ enters solely
through $p^*$, which is normalised away in the sum.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Why the cap is one-sided]
$z \to z_{\mathrm{raw}}$ as $z_{\mathrm{raw}} \to -\infty$, so the floor is
exactly $0$ and the quiet regime is undistorted. An earlier symmetric variant
imposed $\lambda \ge s\,\softplus(b - c)$, which welded the quiet floor to the
burst scale and produced a quiet-regime deficit. The asymmetry --- ceiling for
simulation stability, zero floor for prediction --- is deliberate.
\end{remark}

\section{PCT-S2P2}

The Shi--Cartlidge parallel-per-type construction on a latent linear Hawkes
backbone: $K$ towers of $L$ diagonalised layers, tower $k$'s top output
decoding only $\lambda_k$.

\subsection{Per-tower dynamics}

Per layer $\ell$ and tower $k$: eigenvalues
$\Lambda^{(\ell)}_k \in \C^{P}$ parameterised as
$\Lambda_{k,r} = -e^{\nu_{k,r}} + i\,\theta_{k,r}$ so $\Real \Lambda < 0$,
with $\tilde E^{(\ell)}_k \in \C^{P\times H}$,
$\tilde B^{(\ell)}_k \in \C^{P\times H}$ ($\ell>0$),
$\tilde C^{(\ell)}_k \in \C^{H\times P}$, $D^{(\ell)}_k \in \R^{H}$.
A shared low-rank mark embedding $\alpha \in \R^{K\times H}$ supplies impulses.

With $\Delta t_i = t_i - t_{i-1}$, the interval scale taken at the
\emph{previous} right limit (which keeps the recurrence coefficients
state-independent),
\begin{align}
s_i &= \softplus\!\big(W^{(\ell)}_\delta\, u^{(\ell-1),R}_{i-1}\big), &&\in \R^{P}_{>0}\\
\bar a_i &= \exp\!\big(s_i \odot \Lambda^{(\ell)}_k\, \Delta t_i\big), \\
x^{L}_i &= \bar a_i\, x^{R}_{i-1}
   + (\bar a_i - 1)\,\tilde B^{(\ell)}_k u^{(\ell-1),L}_i,
   && \text{backward ZOH} \label{eq:zoh}\\
x^{R}_i &= x^{L}_i + \tilde E^{(\ell)}_k\, \varepsilon_i^{(k)},
   && \text{event jump}
\end{align}
so that $x^R_i = \bar a_i x^R_{i-1} + b_i$ with
$b_i = (\bar a_i - 1)\tilde B u^L_i + \tilde E \varepsilon_i$ independent of
$x$, and the sequence is computed by a Hillis--Steele associative scan in
$O(\log N)$ depth.

The impulse $\varepsilon^{(k)}_i \in \R^H$ selects the cross-excitation regime:
\begin{equation}
\varepsilon^{(k)}_i =
\begin{cases}
\dfrac{1}{|x_i|}\sum_j x_{i,j}\,\alpha_j & \texttt{impulse\_mode = all}
  \quad\text{(every event jumps every tower)}\\[6pt]
x_{i,k}\,\alpha_k & \texttt{impulse\_mode = own}
  \quad\text{(tower $k$ sees only type $k$)} .
\end{cases}
\end{equation}

\subsection{Between-layer mixing}

Layer outputs at both limits are
$y^{L/R}_k = 2\,\Real\big(\tilde C^{(\ell)}_k x^{L/R}\big) + D^{(\ell)}_k u^{(\ell-1),L/R}$,
and the towers exchange information \emph{between layers} through a shared map
$W_{\mathrm{mix}}^{(\ell)} \in \R^{KH \times KH}$:
\begin{equation}
u^{(\ell)} = \LN\!\Big(\operatorname{GELU}\big(W^{(\ell)}_{\mathrm{mix}}
\operatorname{vec}(y)\big) + \operatorname{vec}(u^{(\ell-1)})\Big),
\qquad L \ge 2 .
\label{eq:mix}
\end{equation}

\begin{remark}[Why mixing is not at event instants]
The original PCT-LSTM makes the post-event state of unit $k$ a function of all
units' left limits. For an LSTM that is free; here it would make $b_i$ depend
on $x_{i-1}$, break the linear recurrence and force $O(N)$ sequential
evaluation. Mixing between layers instead keeps $b_i$ state-independent, so the
parallel scan survives; cross-tower information arrives at the same timestamp,
one layer deeper. Within a layer the tower states never see each other, which
is why $L \ge 2$ is required. The coupled signal enters through the ZOH drift
term of~\eqref{eq:zoh}, acting continuously across the interval, whereas
event-instant jumps carry only $\tilde E \varepsilon$.
\end{remark}

\subsection{Per-type capped head}

Tower $k$'s top-layer left-limit stream $u_k \in \R^H$ decodes only
$\lambda_k$, through the SS2P2 cap of~\eqref{eq:ss2p2rate} applied per tower
with shared gate and readout and a per-type scale $s_k$:
\begin{equation}
\boxed{\;
\lambda_k(t) = s_k\,\softplus\!\big(c - \softplus(c - w^{\!\top}(o_k \odot \tanh u_k) - b)\big) + 10^{-9},
\qquad
\Lambda(t) = \sum_{k=1}^{K}\lambda_k(t),
\qquad
p(k\mid\Hist_t) = \frac{\lambda_k(t)}{\Lambda(t)} . \;}
\label{eq:pcthead}
\end{equation}

\begin{proposition}[Exact dominating rate]
\label{prop:pctbound}
$\Lambda(t) \le \sum_{k=1}^{K} s_k\,\softplus(c) + K\cdot 10^{-9}$ for all $t$
and all histories.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Apply Proposition~\ref{prop:ss2p2bound} per tower and sum.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[No rate neutrality]
\label{prop:pctnotneutral}
There is no non-trivial partition of the parameters into rate-only and
mark-only blocks: every parameter that changes $p(\cdot\mid\Hist_t)$ also
changes $\Lambda(t)$, except along directions that rescale all $\lambda_k$
equally.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
By~\eqref{eq:pcthead}, $p$ and $\Lambda$ are both functions of the same vector
$(\lambda_1,\dots,\lambda_K)$. Fix $t$ and perturb $\lambda \mapsto \lambda +
\eta$. Then $\Lambda$ is unchanged iff $\sum_k \eta_k = 0$, and $p$ is
unchanged iff $\eta \parallel \lambda$. The two conditions hold simultaneously
only if $\eta = 0$, since $\lambda_k > 0$ implies $\sum_k \lambda_k \ne 0$.
Hence no perturbation moves $p$ while fixing $\Lambda$ unless it is orthogonal
to $\mathbf 1$, and no perturbation moves $\Lambda$ while fixing $p$ unless it
is parallel to $\lambda$; the mark and rate objectives therefore compete for
the same parameters.
\end{proof}

\section{Structural comparison}

\begin{table}[h]
\centering
\begin{tabular}{lll}
\toprule
& \textbf{SS2P2} & \textbf{PCT-S2P2} \\
\midrule
backbone & S2P2, real diagonal, shared $H$ & $K$ towers, complex diagonal LLH \\
cross-type coupling & implicit in shared latent & towers + between-layer mixer \\
intensity & $\lambda_k = \Lambda(u)\,p^*_k(u)$ & $\lambda_k$ per tower, $\Lambda = \sum_k \lambda_k$ \\
dominating rate & $s\,\softplus(c)$ & $\sum_k s_k \softplus(c)$ \\
rate neutrality & yes (Prop.~\ref{prop:ss2p2neutral}) & \textbf{no} (Prop.~\ref{prop:pctnotneutral}) \\
destination attribution & not separable & exact ($\lambda_k$ from tower $k$) \\
scan complexity & $O(\log N)$ & $O(\log N)$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\caption{PCT-S2P2 inherits SS2P2's \emph{cap} but not its \emph{factorisation}.}
\end{table}

\begin{remark}[Bounded is not anchored]
Propositions~\ref{prop:ss2p2bound} and~\ref{prop:pctbound} give both models a
ceiling, which is what exact thinning requires; in $18$ PCT-S2P2 rollouts the
ceiling was never violated. But a ceiling constrains only how large $\Lambda$
may become, not where it sits. In SS2P2 the level is held by
Proposition~\ref{prop:ss2p2neutral}: fitting the marks cannot move it. In
PCT-S2P2, Proposition~\ref{prop:pctnotneutral} says the mark and time terms
of~\eqref{eq:ll} compete for the same parameters, and since the mark term
carries $\sim\!3.5$ nats/event over $62$ classes it dominates. The level is
then free to drift.
\end{remark}

\section{Measured consequence}

Twelve epochs, three assets, three impulse regimes, Coinbase 7-day streams.
Let $u_i = \int_{t_{i-1}}^{t_i}\Lambda(v)\,dv$ be the compensator-rescaled
gaps, which satisfy $\E[u] = 1$ under a correctly specified model.

\begin{table}[h]
\centering
\begin{tabular}{lrrrr}
\toprule
& epoch 1 & epoch 12 & SS2P2-family ref & \\
\midrule
validation loss & $-1449$ & $-4214$ & --- & monotone $\downarrow$\\
$\E[u]$ (target $1.0$) & $1.43$ & $2.76$ & $1.08$ & drifts $\uparrow$\\
time-rescaling KS & $0.154$ & $0.492$ & $0.213$ & degrades\\
mark perplexity & $35.5$ & $25.6$ & $24.0$ & improves\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\caption{PCT-S2P2 on SOL, \texttt{impulse\_mode=all}. The objective improves
monotonically while the rate calibration degrades: the model is correctly
trained on a likelihood that does not constrain $\E[u]$. Checkpoint selection
on validation loss therefore returns the \emph{worst}-calibrated epoch.}
\end{table}

At rollout, sim-time calibration fitted on the validation split verified to
within $3.2\%$ but transferred to the test split at $+392\%$; realised rates
were $3.4$--$12\times$ real across all nine arms, with seed-to-seed spread
$\le 1.05\times$ (systematic bias, not sampling noise). Prediction, by
contrast, was unaffected: the ablation ordered
$\texttt{own} > \texttt{all} > \texttt{ind}$ on all three assets, with
cross-type coupling worth $27$--$48\%$ in perplexity.

\begin{remark}[The design choice this forces]
Per-type intensities and structural rate anchoring are incompatible as posed:
one may keep $\lambda_k$ per tower and add an explicit calibration term to the
objective (a penalty on the moments of $u$), or restore
$\lambda_k = \Lambda(u)\,p_k(u)$ and recover
Proposition~\ref{prop:ss2p2neutral} at the cost of per-type rates. A third
route factors the head as
$\lambda_k = \Lambda_{\mathrm{SS2P2}}(u)\cdot\operatorname{softmax}_k(\text{tower outputs})$,
anchoring the level while the towers shape composition --- but the cross-type
structure is then compositional rather than in the rate.
\end{remark}
